# ASH-V website

Next.js 15 marketing site for ASH-V. See README.md for structure, pages and launch notes.

## UX writing (mandatory)

All user-facing text follows `.claude/skills/ash-v-ux-writing/SKILL.md`: the ASH-V Enterprise UX Writing Guidelines,
glossary, capitalization rules and status vocabulary. Load it before writing or editing any copy, and review every new
or modified component against its checklist before considering work complete.

These guidelines take precedence over the copy rules in `.claude/skills/landing-page-design` (its "no hyphens" and
"sentence case for headings and buttons" rules do not apply). The landing-page-design visual system (type scale,
spacing, radius, motion, Phosphor icons) still applies.

## Conventions

- Module scope is defined in `lib/modules.ts`. Never describe a capability it does not support.
- Sample data in visuals is labelled "Sample data".
- Public statistics and regulatory references link to their primary source.
